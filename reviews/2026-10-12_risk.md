### 3. Strongest case against
The trade is a short into an earnings call where management sets the narrative, the moment the stock is breaking out of a 52-week low on heavy volume. On 2026-10-09 CCI gapped up 7.88% at the open, the largest open gap since 2015, and closed +15.60%, also the largest close-to-close gain since 2015. Volume was 14.39M shares against 2.76M to 4.75M in the prior 10 sessions. The proposal's catalyst is mostly the absence of news. If the 2026-10-21 call is silent on SpaceX, nothing forces the price down, and the trade drifts to a near-flat planned exit on 2026-10-23. If management says anything constructive (talks, applications, FCC timeline), the stock can gap through 85.00 overnight. The payoff around the print is skewed against the short: silence gives a small or zero gain, constructive talk gives a gap loss. The stop is also not well clear of current noise. Over the last 252 days, CCI's 9-day high reached +6.73% above the starting close in 13.6% of windows (12.2% since 2015). A reflection-principle estimate at the 63d vol gives 38.8% for a 9-day touch. The true figure for this hold sits somewhere between, because it includes an earnings print in a post-shock regime.

### 4. Pre-mortem (3 months out, the trade lost money)

A rates rally (beta to TLT 0.44) or continued SpaceX-tower enthusiasm lifted AMT, SBAC and CCI together, the daily high crossed 85.00 before or on the print, and the short stopped at -$2,692. The position was a short of the tower factor, not of a CCI-specific mispricing.
