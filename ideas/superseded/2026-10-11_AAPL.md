## Variant view

AAPL fell from 333.43 (2026-07-30) to 308.91 (2026-07-31) after the July call, then recovered to 340.42 on 2026-10-08, 0.2% below its 52-week closing high of 341.07 (prices.py). Ming-Chi Kuo expects any near-term margin hit to be viewed as temporary, with services backfilling [S4].
- where it may be wrong: cost pressure rises after the September quarter instead of fading.
  - On the July 30 call, the CFO attributed more than all of the September-quarter gross margin decline to memory costs. Gross margin guidance was 47% to 48% [S1][S2].
  - The CFO said the inventory cushion shrinks after September. Tim Cook called memory pricing "a hundred-year flood" and said market pricing keeps rising past September [S1][S2].
  - TrendForce estimates the 256GB iPhone 18 Pro bill of materials is about 38% higher than its predecessor's [S5].
  - Nikkei (via [S6][S7]) reported on 2026-10-09 that Apple asked some suppliers to cut October component orders for iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max by at least 15%, citing softer demand after a $100 price increase (Pro $1,199, Pro Max $1,299) [S8].
  - Bernstein models December-quarter iPhone gross margin at 40.4%, 390 bp below consensus, and total gross margin at 46.2%, 50 bp below consensus [S3].
  - Taken together: the December-quarter guide could show gross margin and iPhone growth below what a price near the 52-week high assumes.
- the number that settles it: Apple's December-quarter gross margin guidance range given on the FQ4 call. Bernstein's figures imply a consensus of about 46.7% (Desk arithmetic from [S3]: 46.2% + 0.5 pt). A guided midpoint below roughly 46.7% supports the idea.

## Strongest case it is already priced in
- The margin pressure is public. Apple guided it in July, the stock fell 7.4% on 2026-07-31 (prices.py), and Bernstein's 2026-09-25 note is public [S3].
- AAPL fell only 1.1% on the Nikkei report. Nikkei production-cut stories recur most autumns; MacDailyNews calls it an annual "hit piece" [S6]. The report relies on unnamed supply-chain sources, and Apple has not confirmed it [S7].
- The launch is staggered: the base iPhone 18 and an updated iPhone Air are slated for early 2027 [S6]. A softer December quarter may be read as timing.
- TrendForce expects Apple to absorb part of the cost to protect shipment volume [S5]. A known, deliberate margin trade-off may not surprise the market.
- Short interest is about 1% of shares [S12]. There is little short-covering fuel either way, and positioning is long.
