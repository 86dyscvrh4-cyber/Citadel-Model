## Strongest case it is already priced in
- The bear case runs over years. A clean Q3 print does not answer it, and the 10.8% drop may be a permanent multiple reset rather than an overreaction. Wells Fargo's Steven Cahall said a three-player market gaining a smaller fourth player is "still a net negative, for sure" [S7, snippet only].
- AT&T carries more debt after the EchoStar spectrum deal. AT&T agreed to pay about $23B for EchoStar's 600 MHz and 3.45 GHz licenses [S2]. On 2026-07-28 it drew $14.5B of term loans to fund part of the price [S9]. Total debt was $144.0B and net debt $126.4B at the end of Q2 [S1]. Less balance-sheet room limits how much repurchases can support the stock.
- More supply is coming. The FCC adopted rules on 2026-07-22 to auction upper C-band spectrum by July 2027 [S10, snippet only]. That is after this run, but it is a known overhang.
- VZ and TMUS fell further than T on the same day (prices.py history). If the market sees T as the least exposed, the relative rebound case may already be in the price.
