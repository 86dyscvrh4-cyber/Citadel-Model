The analyst figure in hand is a 2026 FFO consensus of $4.54 per share (earningswhispers via search [S7], snippet only, date of estimate unknown). - where it may be wrong: the one-day gain already pays for more than a full new national tenant. That tenant has not signed, has not committed to build, and cannot deploy until the FCC approves the deal.
  - On 425,455,439 shares [S1], the 10.75 move added about $4.57B of market value in one day (Desk arithmetic).
  - The last national new-entrant tenant was DISH. Its terminated master lease had more than $3.5B of remaining payments [S1]. Losing it cuts about $220M from 2026 site rental revenue year over year [S1]. CCI terminated the DISH MLA on 2026-01-12 for non-payment, and DISH filed Chapter 11 on 2026-06-30 [S1].
  - Taking $220M a year at the pre-move 15.0x AFFO multiple gives about $3.3B (Desk arithmetic; this overstates value because it treats revenue as AFFO). The $4.57B one-day gain is above that and above the >$3.5B of contracted DISH payments.
  - CCI earned about 93% of site rental revenue for the first half of 2026 from T-Mobile, AT&T and Verizon [S1]. Those three tenants fell 10.1% to 13.3% on the same news (prices.py history). If SpaceX takes share, it does so from CCI's paying tenants.
  - Analysts still say SpaceX needs an MVNO deal, and the spectrum may serve as leverage for one rather than as a build plan [S6, snippet only]. An MVNO deal adds no tower leases.
- the number that settles it: CCI's Q3 2026 release and call. Watch whether the 2026 outlook for site rental billings ($3,805M to $3,835M) and organic contribution (about $135M, or 3.4% at the midpoint, ex DISH and Sprint) [S2] changes, and whether management reports any SpaceX lease application, LOI or MLA. ## Strongest case it is already priced in
- The move may be a rerating from a depressed base, not a SpaceX-specific bet. CCI was at a 52-week low on 2026-10-01, and at 79.64 it is still about 19% below its 52-week high (prices.py). A 17.4x AFFO multiple may not be stretched against CCI's own history, but Desk did not retrieve that history (UNVERIFIED).
- SpaceX is not DISH. It paid about $8B in cash for the licenses [S5, snippet only], so tenant credit risk is lower and the DISH comparison may undervalue a SpaceX lease program.
- If SpaceX builds on low-band spectrum, it needs macro towers. KeyBanc and JPMorgan said so on the day [S6, snippet only].
- AMT (+9.3%) and SBAC (+7.3%) moved less (prices.py). CCI's larger move may reflect its pure US exposure, which is the right exposure if a US build happens.
- The Q3 print may not mention SpaceX at all. The thesis then rests on the absence of news, and the price can hold while the FCC review runs past this run.
