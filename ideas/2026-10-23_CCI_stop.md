- User instruction 07:15 ET: "Cover the cci short at the open". Scheduled for after the 09:30 bar.
- What happened: CCI opened at 91.45 (Yahoo 1-minute bar 09:30 ET: open 91.4495, high 91.4495, low 89.90, close 90.31), above the 85.00 stop. Prior close 78.83.
- Cause (news, snippet only): Bloomberg report that SpaceX seeks access to at least 80,000 US cell sites and is in direct talks with Crown Castle; AMT also up about 11% premarket. https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/premarket-movers-crown-castle-jumps-75-on-spacex-talks-autoliv-slides-4969100 | retrieved 2026-10-23 | snippet only. No CCI 8-K since 2026-10-21 (scripts/sec.py).
- Which exit governs: rules.md section 7. The stop is checked on the daily bar; the bar gapped through the stop, so it fills at the open. The stop triggers at the opening print, before the 09:30 bar closes, so the user-requested exit (09:30 bar close, 90.31) was not recorded. Recording 90.31 would book a better price than the rules allow.
- Booking: scripts/mark_to_market.py books the stop at the Yahoo daily open after the 16:30 ET close (completed bars only). Expected P&L at 91.45: about -$5,475 (0.55% of capital). Planned loss at 85.00 was -$2,268; the gap made it 2.4x. Risk's 10-12 review named this exact path ("constructive talk gives a gap loss").
- Lesson for the postmortem: the gap came at the 10-23 open, so neither the planned exit (10-23 close) nor the user's hold decision changed the outcome. The exposure was holding two sessions past the print on a thesis of "SpaceX has signed nothing": a report of talks, not a signed lease, was enough to gap the stock 16%. A stop does not cap the loss of a short into a known pending headline.
